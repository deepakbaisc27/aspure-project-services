Category 01

Electrical Works

Complete electrical installation and erection services for industrial, municipal, and commercial projects — executed to IS code standards with certified personnel.

1.1

HT/LT Panel Installation

Installation of High-Tension and Low-Tension switchgear panels, MCC panels, PCC panels, and distribution boards at industrial and municipal project sites.

  • HT Switchgear & VCB Panels
  • MCC (Motor Control Centre) Panels
  • PCC (Power Control Centre) Panels
  • Distribution Boards (DB/SMDB)
  • AMF & DG Set Panels
  • Bus Duct & Bus Bar Installation
1.2

Cable Laying & Termination

Supply and erection of HT/LT power cables, control cables, and instrumentation cables — including cable tray, conduit routing, and crimping termination.

  • HT Power Cable (11kV / 33kV)
  • LT Power Cable Laying
  • Control & Signal Cable Works
  • Cable Tray & Conduit Routing
  • Cable Crimping & Termination
  • Cable Ferrule & Glanding
1.3

Earthing & Lightning Protection

Design and installation of complete earthing systems and lightning protection networks for electrical safety compliance at industrial and outdoor project sites.

  • Plate Earthing & Pipe Earthing
  • Equipment Body Earthing
  • Grid Earthing for Substations
  • Lightning Arrestor Installation
  • Earth Continuity Testing
  • Surge Protection Devices (SPD)
1.4

Pumps Installation

Electrical works for pump installation including motor wiring, starter connection, DOL/VFD drive connections, and pump control panel integration.

  • Submersible & Centrifugal Pumps
  • Motor Winding & Connections
  • DOL / Star-Delta Starter Wiring
  • VFD / Soft Starter Connections
  • Pump Control Panel Wiring
  • Auto-Manual Control Integration
Category 02

Instrumentation Works

Advanced instrumentation installation, calibration, and integration services for process control and automation systems in water treatment, industrial, and utility projects.

2.1

Instruments Installation

Field installation of all types of process instruments including transmitters, sensors, flow meters, level instruments, and pressure gauges.

  • Pressure Transmitters & Gauges
  • Flow Meters (Electromagnetic, Ultrasonic)
  • Level Sensors & Transmitters
  • Temperature Sensors (RTD / TC)
  • Analyzers (pH, DO, Turbidity)
  • Control Valves & Actuators
2.2

Calibration Services

Precision calibration of all field instruments to ensure accurate measurement, proper signal output, and process control performance.

  • Pressure Instrument Calibration
  • Temperature Instrument Calibration
  • Flow Meter Zero / Span Setting
  • Level Instrument Calibration
  • Positioner Calibration (Control Valves)
  • Calibration Record & Documentation
2.3

Loop Checking

End-to-end loop verification from field instruments to control panels — ensuring correct signal transmission, wiring continuity, and proper I/O mapping.

  • Continuity & Insulation Testing
  • 4–20 mA Signal Verification
  • Digital I/O Loop Testing
  • Alarm & Trip Loop Checks
  • Field-to-Panel Signal Tracing
  • Loop Check Sheets Documentation
2.4

PLC / SCADA Integration

PLC panel wiring, I/O testing, and SCADA system integration for process automation — including field signal connection and communication testing.

  • PLC Panel Internal Wiring
  • I/O Card Installation & Testing
  • Field-to-PLC Loop Testing
  • SCADA Screen Signal Verification
  • Communication Wiring (PROFIBUS, Modbus)
  • HMI / SCADA Functional Testing
Category 03

Testing & Commissioning

Comprehensive pre-commissioning checks, system testing, and final commissioning of all electrical and instrumentation systems — ensuring safe and reliable operation from day one.

3.1

Transformer Testing

Complete pre-commissioning and commissioning tests for power transformers and distribution transformers to verify performance before energization.

  • IR (Insulation Resistance) Test
  • Turns Ratio Test (TTR)
  • Winding Resistance Test
  • Oil BDV (Dielectric Strength) Test
  • Vector Group Verification
  • No-Load & Load Loss Test
3.2

Relay Testing

Testing, calibration, and setting of protection relays including overcurrent, earth fault, differential, and bus bar protection relays.

  • Overcurrent Relay (OCR) Testing
  • Earth Fault Relay (EFR) Testing
  • Differential Relay Testing
  • Buchholz & OTI/WTI Relay Check
  • Trip Circuit Verification
  • Relay Setting & Coordination
3.3

Pre-Commissioning Checks

Systematic pre-commissioning inspection and testing to verify all electrical and instrumentation works are complete, correct, and safe before energization.

  • Insulation Resistance Testing (IR)
  • Continuity & Polarity Checks
  • Cable Hi-Pot (HV) Testing
  • Panel Internal Wiring Inspection
  • Earthing System Testing
  • Motor Rotation & Meg Test
3.4

Final Commissioning

Full system energization, functional testing, and trial run of electrical and instrumentation systems — ensuring all systems operate as designed.

  • HT/LT System Energization
  • Motor Trial Run & Load Testing
  • Pump System Functional Test
  • Instrumentation System Live Check
  • SCADA / PLC Live Signal Test
  • Commissioning Report Preparation
Our Approach

How We Deliver Every Project
