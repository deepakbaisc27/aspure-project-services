Company Background

Who We Are & What We Do

Aspure Project Services is a registered Electrical & Instrumentation contracting firm headquartered in Prem Parisar, Patalpul Bhilai, Chhattisgarh. Founded with a vision to deliver precision-engineered electrical and instrumentation solutions, we have grown into a trusted partner for EPC contractors, municipal bodies, and industrial clients across India.

We specialize in the complete scope of electrical and instrumentation works — from HT/LT panel installation, cable laying and termination, earthing and lightning protection systems, instruments installation and calibration, PLC/SCADA installation and comprehensive testing & commissioning services.

Our team of experienced engineers, supervisors, and skilled technicians has successfully executed projects in water treatment plants, pumping stations, industrial facilities, and commercial infrastructure — always delivering on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards.
